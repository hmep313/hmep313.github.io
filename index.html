Biography

I am a full-time Research Assistant at The University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Lequan Yu. My research interests lie in continual learning, diffusion models, and AI agents, with a particular interest in medical applications.

I received my B.E. in Biomedical Engineering from Xi’an Jiaotong University in June 2026. Previously, I worked with Prof. Hong Wang and Dr. Yichen Wu on medical image class incremental learning, and with Prof. Sen Lin on optimizer design for online continual learning.

Research Interest

  • Continual Learning: Class incremental learning and online continual learning, with a focus on the stability–plasticity dilemma.
  • Diffusion Models: Pixel-space diffusion for high-fidelity text rendering and fine-grained visual content.
  • AI Agents: Evidence-guided medical agents that integrate visual inspection, medical knowledge, and localization feedback.
